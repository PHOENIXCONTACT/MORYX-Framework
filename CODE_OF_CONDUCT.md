# Code of Conduct

The MORYX Framework community is committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and professional environment for everyone participating in the project.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that supports a positive community include:

- Being friendly, patient, and considerate.
- Respecting differing opinions, experiences, and levels of expertise.
- Giving and accepting constructive technical feedback.
- Focusing discussions on the project and resolving disagreements constructively.
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or threats.
- Insulting, derogatory, or discriminatory comments or jokes.
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Trolling, personal attacks, spam, or deliberate disruption.
- Intentionally compromising the security of the project or its users.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional environment.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all MORYX Framework community spaces, including issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other project communication. It also applies when an individual officially represents the project in public or at an online or in-person event.

In addition, the project is maintained in accordance with the [Code of Conduct of the Phoenix Contact Group](https://www.phoenixcontact.com/assets/6c048e8f-7c32-470a-b1d0-421f95adef2d/epaper/1183074_EN_Code_of_Conduct_LoR.pdf). Where applicable, its principles supplement this community-focused Code of Conduct.

## Reporting and enforcement

Use the appropriate private reporting channel:

- For community behavior in MORYX project spaces, contact [moryx@phoenixcontact.com](mailto:moryx@phoenixcontact.com).
- For concerns involving the Code of Conduct of the Phoenix Contact Group, use the [Phoenix Contact whistleblowing system](https://www.phoenixcontact.com/en-pc/whistleblowing-system).

Do not disclose sensitive reports in a public issue, pull request, or discussion.

Project maintainers will review reports in good faith, respect the privacy and security of those involved as far as reasonably possible, and respond in a manner proportionate to the circumstances. Appropriate action may include a warning, removal of content, temporary restriction, or permanent exclusion from project spaces.

Maintainers who are directly involved in a report should not participate in deciding its outcome.
